# Wrapping up

You have used most of Sage now. This last step tours the settings, tidies up the playground and points to where to go next.

## Try it: the settings tour

Open the settings with the **⚙** beside the vault's name.

1. **Vault** shows the path of the vault you have open.
2. **Shortcuts** lists every shortcut, including the `Ctrl+Shift+M` you gave your Quick Add entry. The full list is also in [[Keyboard shortcuts]].
3. **Appearance** chooses light, dark, or whatever your system uses.
4. **Quick Add**: remove the two practice entries from [[08 Templates and Quick Add]], or keep them if they are useful.

## Try it: tidy up

1. Right-click the `Playground` folder in the tree and choose **Delete**. Everything in it moves to `.trash`.
2. If **My second note** or a task note from [[06 Task documents]] ended up outside `Playground`, delete those the same way.

## Try it: finish the tutorial

1. Mark this step `status: done`, and any step you have not marked yet.
2. Open [[finish-the-sage-tutorial]] and change its `status` to `done`. Every link to it, here and in [[01 Welcome]], now says *[Done]*.

## Where next

- The [[Sage guide]] lists every reference page.
- [[Keyboard shortcuts]] is worth a second look.
- To use Sage on your own notes, run **Open vault folder…** from the [[Command palette]] and choose your vault. An Obsidian vault works as it is; Sage keeps its index outside the vault and its few settings in `.sage/settings.json`. See [[Getting started]].
- If your vault lives in OneDrive or is edited by other apps at the same time, read [[External changes and merging]].

Happy writing.
