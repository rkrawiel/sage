# Welcome to Sage

This tutorial teaches Sage by using it. Each step is a note like this one: read it, try what it asks, then follow the link to the next step. It takes about half an hour.

Your progress is tracked by the task [[finish-the-sage-tutorial]]. Open it and change its `status` to `in-progress` when you are ready to begin.

## What you will learn

1. **Welcome**: the window and this tutorial (you are here)
2. [[02 Your first note]]: creating and editing a note
3. [[03 Linking notes]]: wikilinks, autocomplete and backlinks
4. [[04 Tags and properties]]: metadata on notes
5. [[05 Tracking tasks]]: checkbox tasks, due dates, recurring tasks
6. [[06 Task documents]]: whole notes as tasks
7. [[07 Building a dashboard]]: views, task lists and Kanban boards
8. [[08 Templates and Quick Add]]: less typing
9. [[09 Rename, move and delete]]: reorganising safely
10. [[10 Finding things]]: the palette and search everywhere
11. [[11 Wrapping up]]: settings and next steps

Everything you make goes into a `Playground` folder, which you throw away in the last step. The reference pages behind each step are listed in the [[Sage guide]].

## Try it: look around

1. Find this note in the **folder tree** on the left: it is in `Tutorial`. Click the arrow beside `Docs` to open that folder too.
2. Press `Ctrl+E` a few times. The tab cycles between **source** (the Markdown you type), **split** and **preview** (the rendered note). Leave it on split for now.
3. In the preview, look at the top of the note: the **properties** of this note, `title`, `type`, `step`, `status` and so on. They come from the YAML block at the top of the source.
4. Scroll to the bottom of the preview. **Linked from** lists the notes that link here, like the [[Sage guide]]; **Links to** lists the notes this one links to.
5. Press `Ctrl+Shift+L` to switch between light and dark, and once more to switch back.

> [!tip] Marking a step done
> When you finish a step, change `status: todo` to `status: done` at the top of its source. The table of steps in the [[Sage guide]] updates by itself. You will see why in [[04 Tags and properties]].
